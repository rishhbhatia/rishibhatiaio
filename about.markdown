I'm Rishi. After a decade building AI and data systems at scale, the lesson
I keep relearning is that better operations don't come from more tools — they
come from owning your data model. Most of my career has been spent proving
that in production.

I started out in tech consulting, quant research, and marketing analytics.
Then I spent several years at Meta as a data engineer building consumer
augmented-reality products — pipelines, platforms, and relevance systems at a
scale few companies ever see. Since then I've worked with AI and data
startups, from standing up a venture-backed game studio's first analytics
platform as its Head of Data & Analytics to running
[Ceroh](https://www.ceroh.ai), where I build AI and data systems for media
agencies. The two projects I'm proudest of are written up as
[case studies](/work/).

The work I keep choosing has a shape: take something that exists only as a
model, an idea, or a pile of raw data, and turn it into a system other people
can rely on without me in the room.

When my laptop is closed, I'm strength training, swimming, or snowboarding —
and my favorite: 1:1 conversations with fascinating people in cafés all over
San Francisco. If you're one of them, say hi.
